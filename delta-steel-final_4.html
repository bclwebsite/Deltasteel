Welcome to Delta Steel

Welcome to Delta Steel
Re-Rolling Mills

Delta Steel Re-Rolling Mills (India) is a leading manufacturer and supplier of premium structural steel sections based in Pilwai, Mehsana, Gujarat. Established in 2022, we operate a modern rolling mill producing angle bars, I-beams and channel sections to IS:2062 standards.

We supply dealers, contractors and fabricators across Gujarat and pan-India, always from ready stock. Our signature blue-mark quality system ensures every section is traceable and verified before dispatch.
